## Found and fixed in this pass

**The mobile screenshot-crop bug (originally flagged P1 by Assessment A) was verified and fixed before this snapshot was written**, following this project's established convention of resolving same-session regressions rather than leaving them open. Root cause: tonight's earlier fix (aspect-ratio → 4:3) changed the *ratio value* but left `height` unset on the real `<img>` elements, which still carry old HTML `width`/`height` attributes (e.g. `width="1200" height="620"`). Since `aspect-ratio` only fills in a dimension that's `auto`, and `height` was pinned to a fixed px value by the attribute (not `auto`), the box rendered squashed — at 390px the case-hero-visual box measured 350×620 (ratio 0.56, portrait) instead of 4:3, and `object-fit: cover` zoomed in far enough to behead on-screen headline text ("Restore Your Surfaces with Confidence" rendered as "e Your Surfaces / onfidence") on every services/work-index/case-hero surface with a real screenshot. This is the exact mechanism last night's critique originally diagnosed for the prior P0. Added `height: auto` to the five affected rules (`.case-hero-visual img`, `.built-shot img`, `.work-visual img`, `.proof-visual img` ×2, `.claim-visual img`). Verified: computed rendered ratio is now exactly 1.333 on all five at 390px, and a direct visual crop confirms the headline text renders intact.

## Overall Impression

This is a real design system executed with unusual discipline, and its best surfaces — /contact and /studios/church-studio — are close to unimpeachable: distinct section archetypes, honest copy, proof that leads. Tonight's fixes closed the gap between the site's chrome and its craft layer on several fronts (headings, testimonials, closing CTA, footer, and now the image-crop mechanism itself). What's left is concentrated in three places: the conversion layer (both forms cannot currently succeed — a pre-existing, already-tracked blocker, not introduced tonight), navigation that promises destinations that don't exist, and a genuinely broken mobile accordion. None of these were touched by tonight's six phases; all three predate this session.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Both conversion forms are guaranteed to fail.** `src/pages/contact.astro` and `src/pages/studios/church-studio.astro` both ship `value="YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY"`. Every submission of the site's only two conversion actions errors. Why it matters: the peak-end of both journeys is currently a failure state, and this is the highest-traffic conversion path on the site. Fix: paste the real Web3Forms public access key (already tracked in plan.md's open items — not new, but now the site's clearest single blocker now that the image P0 is resolved). Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`.

**[P1] Nav promises two destinations that don't exist.** `Header.astro` links "Pricing" → `/#pricing` and "Blog" → `/#blog`; `Footer.astro`'s "Read the Blog →" does the same. No `id="pricing"` or `id="blog"` exists anywhere in `src/`. Why it matters: the primary persona's stated buying criterion is "clear pricing, agreed upfront" — the nav's Pricing link is the exact moment a skeptical buyer verifies that promise, and it silently fails. Fix: remove both until the pages exist (lane rule: wire only routes that exist), or ship them. Suggested command: `/impeccable audit` (surfaces it), `/impeccable clarify` (words the interim fix).

**[P1] Mobile nav accordion is structurally broken.** `src/styles/global.css` line 125: `.mobile-submenu { display: flex; … }` has no `:not([hidden])` guard (unlike `.mobile-menu` just above it, which does), so both submenus render fully expanded the instant the mobile menu opens regardless of the `hidden` attribute or `aria-expanded` state. Verified by source read. Pre-existing; not touched by any of tonight's six phases. Why it matters: the mobile menu becomes a 13-link wall with the primary CTA pushed below the fold. Fix: change the selector to `.mobile-submenu:not([hidden])`. Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`.

**[P2] Branding and Web Design detail pages are near-indistinguishable.** Same template, same two screenshots (smith-cash, legacy), re-worded captions; branding contains zero brand-specific artifacts (no palette, type specimen, logo lockup). Why it matters: a buyer comparing the two pages sees identical evidence and can't tell the disciplines apart, undercutting "prove, don't claim" exactly where the claim is made. Fix: give branding its own proof material. Suggested command: `/impeccable shape`.
