# Pricing Scanability Polish Design

## Goal

Make the opening half of `/pricing` easier to scan without changing its factual claims or replacing Proudly's Grounded Workshop visual world.

## Approved composition

The hero remains centered and cream. "What's always included" becomes a wide two-column fact ledger with four rows, terse labels, supporting sentences, and hairline dividers. It has no numbering, eyebrow labels, floating cards, shadows, or individual background fills.

"What moves the number" becomes an asymmetrical two-column composition. A static Pure White card on the left gives the typical `$4,000–$8,000` band primary authority and places the `$10,000+` complex-project threshold below a hairline. It follows the established light-card grammar with a 1px Hairline Grey edge, soft 16px radius, Espresso type, muted body copy, and no shadow. Five compact factor rows sit on the right with labels and explanations. The surrounding section remains cream.

The white price card is not sticky. At mobile widths, it appears before the factor rows. Care and Growth, FAQ, and inquiry retain their existing structures.

## Copy decisions

The included timeline changes from roughly one month to roughly one week in the canonical pricing draft and route. Other uses of "month" that describe billing or monthly Growth Plan work remain unchanged.

## Responsive and accessibility requirements

- The fact ledger uses two columns on desktop and one column on mobile.
- The price and factor composition uses two columns on desktop and one column on mobile, with price first.
- Existing semantic headings and list relationships remain available to assistive technology.
- Espresso and muted supporting text on Pure White meet WCAG AA contrast.
- No horizontal overflow appears at 390px.

## Scope boundaries

No global component extraction, dependency, animation, form behavior, pricing number, plan term, FAQ answer, or navigation behavior changes in this pass.
