## 2. Who It's For

### Built for Churches Ready to Take Their Website Seriously

Church Studio works best for:

- One pastor or a small team making the decision — not a committee
- A church that already has real momentum: consistent attendance, active ministries, a clear identity
- Leadership who wants the site to serve first-time visitors, not just members
- Churches who already use Planning Center, Church Center, or a giving platform and want the site to work alongside them, not replace them

If your church is just getting started or design decisions run through a large board, a slower and more custom process — or simply more time before a rebuild — usually serves you better.

Every Church Studio project starts at $700. Two things move the price up within that range:

- **Number of ministries** — more distinct ministries beyond the core structure means more content to organize and present clearly.
- **Multisite** — if your church has more than one campus, each location needs its own service times, address, and directions built into the site.

A single-campus church with a typical ministry list lands at the low end. Reach out and I'll give you an exact number based on your church.

## 8. FAQ

**How is this different from a full Proudly Sites project?**
Church Studio uses a fixed page structure and layout system built specifically for churches, instead of a fully custom design process. You still get original copy and a site that looks like your church, just built on a faster timeline.

**Do you write the copy?**
Yes. I'll ask the right questions during onboarding — your service length, what to wear, your kids' check-in process, whether you livestream — and write copy that sounds like your pastor, not a denominational press release.

**Can I still link out to Planning Center, Church Center, or our giving platform?**
Yes. Church Studio is built to work alongside the tools you already use, not replace them.

**What if we need more than the eight pages?**
The eight-page structure covers what most churches need. If you have a real case for more — a students ministry, multiple locations — let's talk. It may move you toward the top of the pricing range or into a standard Proudly Sites project instead.

**Who updates the site after launch?**
You will, or whoever on your team you choose. The site is built so your team can update service times, events, and series art without touching code.
